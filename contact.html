Contact Us

We would love to hear from you.

For support, feedback, feature ideas or any question about My Statement, send us an email. We usually reply within 2 to 3 working days.

support.gkadda@gmail.com

What to include

  • Your registered email address
  • Your branch name (never send your password or branch code)
  • A short description of the problem, and a screenshot if possible

Common questions

I forgot my branch code. Ask your branch admin. The branch code is shared by the admin with delivery boys.

I entered something wrong in a request. Ask your admin to reject the request, then send a corrected one.

I want my data removed. Email us from your registered address and we will help. See our Privacy Policy for details.
