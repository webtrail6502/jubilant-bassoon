Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information My Statement collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you register and use the app, we collect:

  • Account details: your name, email address and branch code.
  • Records you enter: daily statements, amounts collected and paid, cash denomination counts, PhonePe amounts, delivery and pickup counts, and the photo you attach to a denomination request.
  • Basic technical data: browser type and similar information that is automatically sent when you visit any website.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show your records to you and to your branch admin.
  • To keep the service secure and working properly.
  • To reply to messages you send us.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who can see your data

Data is separated by branch code. Delivery boys can see only their own submissions and the denomination history of their branch. The branch admin can see the statements and requests of that branch. People outside the branch cannot see it.

4. Where data is stored

Account and record data is stored and processed by Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. Photos attached to denomination requests are stored along with the request. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in. If you use the public pages of this site, third parties such as Google may also place cookies, as described below.

6. Google advertising (AdSense)

This website may display advertisements served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites on the internet. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this site or other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertisements are shown only on public pages such as this one. They are not shown inside your private account area.

7. Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account and records by emailing us from your registered address.
  • You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time.

8. Children

My Statement is intended for working adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Please see our Contact page.
