About My Statement

A simple way for delivery branches to track every rupee collected and paid, every day.

Why we built it

In many delivery branches, the day's cash is counted by hand and written on paper or in chat messages. Notes get lost, totals are disputed, and the admin has no clear picture of what each rider collected and what has been paid to the company. My Statement replaces that with one shared, always up-to-date record.

What it does

Daily statementsRecord what each person collected and paid. Balances are calculated automatically.
Cash denominationsRiders enter the count of each note and coin, with a photo as proof.
One-tap approvalThe admin accepts or rejects each request, and totals update instantly.
Delivery and pickup countsKeep track of parcels given for delivery and pickups to collect.
PhonePe amountsOnline payments are recorded alongside cash so nothing is missed.
Branch privacyEach branch has its own code. Data is visible only inside that branch.

Who it is for

  • Delivery boys who want to hand in their day quickly and have proof of what they submitted.
  • Branch admins and owners who want to see collected, paid and balance amounts for every date in one place.

How it works

The admin registers and creates a unique branch code. Delivery boys register with that code, then send their denominations each day. The admin reviews each request, approves it, and the branch totals update live on every phone.

Free to use. My Statement works in any modern phone or computer browser. Nothing to install.
